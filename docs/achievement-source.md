# Intended Benefit

Recognise personal records, adherence milestones and phase completion without making training feel like an arcade.

# Product Principle

Recognition should follow a real achievement and explain why it matters. Avoid badges for trivial taps or daily app opening.

# Revisit During

Step 05 — Make the Portal the Source of Truth.

## Approved achievement catalogue — v1

Approved on <date start="2026-11-12" /> following the user's approval of the complete eight-group catalogue. Names, groups and listed thresholds are approved product direction; implementation and qualifying-rule validation remain outstanding. These are ASCEND decisions, not verified Flex AI unlock rules.

### Naming and presentation

Mythic titles are reserved for achievement and phase recognition; always pair them with a plain exercise/target subtitle. Example: <b>Atlas III — Deadlift 140 kg</b>. No points or automatic experience ranks are introduced.

### 1. The Iron Total — combined strength clubs

Combine qualifying barbell back squat, flat barbell bench press and floor deadlift singles. The time window and verification rules remain open.

Store a short evidence explanation. Logs alone do not prove technique or nutrition adherence. Recognition never independently changes the active prescription.

### 8. The Path — signature phase achievements

| Achievement | Existing coaching completion standard |
| --- | --- |
| Tame the Flesh | Stable adherence, repeatable technique, complete logging, basic progression, acceptable recovery and ability to follow the plan without constant rescue; confirmed by Sahmi. |
| Forge the Form | Meaningful physique change, understanding of progression, recovery from structured volume and clarity on what to maintain, build or refine; confirmed by Sahmi. |
| Ascend | Sustainable refined system, honest fatigue management, balanced specialisation and readiness for a new goal cycle; confirmed by Sahmi. |

Phase, training level and organisation stage remain separate. No automatic phase or training-level promotion from time subscribed, badge count, points or load thresholds.

### Progress screen and award integrity

- Preserve primary navigation: Today / Workouts / Nutrition / Progress.
- Place Overview and Achievements within Progress. Achievement filters: The Iron Total, Forge, Rise, Balance, Beyond, The Practice, Mastery, The Path.
- Include Earned, In Progress and All views. Prioritise programme-relevant milestones while allowing catalogue browsing.
- Show title, exact exercise/target, result, date and explanation. Combined-total cards show each contributing lift.
- Use canonical recorded logs; compare the same exercise variation and equipment, exclude warm-ups from performance-record comparisons and never merge substituted movements.
- Recorded achievements and estimated strength remain distinct. Estimated totals do not award a completed-lift club.
- Private by default, optional photos, no new public leaderboards or social feed.
- No missed-session penalties, broken streaks, forced catch-up or deducted points.
- Achievement recognition must not interrupt the main coaching action or encourage departure from the approved programme.

### Remaining implementation decisions and checks

- Define combined-total qualification window, permitted variations, technique standards and evidence/verification process.
- Define acceptable dated-bodyweight window and baseline comparability.
- Define qualifying completed-session rules, rep-record eligibility and data correction/recalculation behaviour.
- Design the award evaluator, coach approvals, duplicate protection, catalogue cards and mobile states; test units, edits, substitutions and estimated-versus-recorded values.
- Confirm exercise-specific execution criteria before live awards. Approval of the catalogue is not a claim of completed implementation.
